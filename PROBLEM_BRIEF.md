# Career Pivot Coach — Problem Brief & Product Strategy

## 1. The Core Problem
When mid-career professionals attempt to switch industries or functional domains, traditional career advice consistently fails them. The failure modes are systematic:

* **Generic, Unactionable Advice:** Candidates are told to "network more," "tailor your resume," or "learn Python," with no specificity regarding which exact skills move the needle for their specific target roles.
* **The "Invisible Experience" Penalty:** Switchers have years of valuable professional experience, but hiring managers and automated applicant tracking systems (ATS) view their past titles through an orthodox lens. A high school teacher's experience with curriculum differentiation, grade tracking, and parent stakeholder alignment is overlooked because it isn't articulated in the lexicon of data analytics (e.g., metric definition, KPI tracking, executive presentation, exploratory data analysis).
* **Honest Feedback Deficit:** Candidates practice interviews in isolation or with polite peers who cannot calibrate answers against an objective hiring rubric. They enter interviews unaware of where they sound hesitant, rambling, or ungrounded in the new domain's vocabulary.
* **Theoretical Knowledge Without Verifiable Proof:** Transitioners complete certificates or read books, but recruiters demand proof of competence through domain-relevant portfolio artifacts.

---

## 2. Target Users
Our users are ambitious professionals making high-stakes, non-linear career pivots:
* **The Educator:** A high school math or science teacher transitioning into a Junior Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer, or Curriculum Product Manager role.
* **The Finance Specialist:** An accountant or financial auditor pivoting to Product Management, BizOps, or FinTech Strategy.
* **The Service/Operations Leader:** A retail store manager, hospitality director, or clinical nurse moving into Customer Success Operations, HealthTech Project Management, or Operations Analysis.

---

## 3. Why Our Approach Fixes It
Career Pivot Coach replaces vague motivation with an empirical, six-stage transition engine:

1. **Speech-to-Profile Cleanup:** Users speak freely about their daily work and responsibilities. The app strips verbal hesitation and filler words, producing a clear, professional narrative.
2. **Transferable Competency Translation:** Past duties are actively mapped into target-domain language with explicit rationale and strength calibration.
3. **Empirical Skill Gap Mapping:** Rather than guessing what employers want, the user inputs real job descriptions from their target market. The engine aggregates skill mentions across postings to distinguish *Critical Gaps* (found in 70%+ of postings) from *Nice-to-Haves*.
4. **Targeted Curriculum & Portfolio Blueprints:** A week-by-week learning plan focuses strictly on the high-frequency gaps. It culminates in a concrete portfolio project that acts as tangible proof of competence.
5. **Calibrated Voice Mock Interviews:** The user practices speaking the new domain's language aloud. Answers are scored against a transparent, public 4-factor rubric (Transferable Evidence, STAR Structure, Domain Fluency, Conciseness) with constructive rewrites.
6. **Outcome-Driven Application Tracking:** An application tracker logs interview callbacks against skill match percentages, empirically learning which types of roles generate real employer interest.
